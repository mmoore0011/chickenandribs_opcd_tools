**New Textures via Materialize (free)**

1) Google an image you want to use as a texture and save   
   1) I try and put everything in the same folder but don’t use anything in your project yet  
2) Materialize  
   1) Watch the tutorial [http://boundingboxsoftware.com/materialize/](http://boundingboxsoftware.com/materialize/) [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1yO0UlLnC4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1yO0UlLnC4)  
   2) Download Materialize and start  
   3) Under Diffuse map hit the O button and find your image file  
   4) Click create heightmap  
      1) Adjust the dials as you like but start with defaults until you get the hang of it  
   5) Click create Normal  
   6) Create metalness  
   7) Create AO  
   8) Click show full material to get an idea of how it looks  
   9) Click tile maps and make sure you are 2048 x 2048\.    
      1) I adjust overlap x and overlap y a bit until I like it  
      2) Set maps  
   10) Check file format PNG   
   11) Save Property Map  
       1) I again save to my texture folder the same as my image  
   12) I also save project so if I want to adjust something I don’t have to start over  
3) Open Quixel Mixer  
   1) Start a new mix  
   2) Click on Library menu and Import Custom Asset  
      1) Load your images created in Materialize to the appropriate boxes  
         1) Duffuse to both Diffuse and Alebdo  
         2) Metalness to Metalness  
         3) AO to AO  
         4) Height to roughness and Displacement  
   3) Click next and fill out the rest of the data  
      1) Adjust your height here a little don’t have it as 0 or it will be flat  
   4) Click your new asset in your library  
   5) Use export tab to export your new texture  
      1) Follow the video by Masta Blasta on how to export\!\!  
         1) Make sure it is saved properly in the right folder of your unity project  
      2) **You do not need to invert your Alpha channel if you do it this way**  
   6) Adjust your Normal in Unity  
   7) Adjust your Arrays

      **ENJOY YOUR TEXTURE\!\!**
